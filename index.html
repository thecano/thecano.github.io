Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Champions damage calculator engine.

How does the calculator handle Pokémon Champions stat formulas?

Pokémon Champions removes IVs, replacing them with direct Stat Points (max 32 per stat, 66 global cap). Our calculator applies the formula automatically, letting you allocate SP and select natures to get 100% accurate stats.

Does this calculator support Mega Evolution?

Yes. This tool fully supports Mega Evolution stats, types, and abilities, allowing you to compute damage rolls for Mega Pokémon during battle simulations.

How do I import my Pokémon Champions sets from Showdown?

Simply copy the export text of a Pokémon from your Showdown team builder. When you open the calculator, the tool automatically detects the set in your clipboard and prompts you to import it in a single tap, mapping all stats, moves, item, and ability to your active slot instantly.

Can I use this calculator on my mobile phone during games?

Absolutely. The web version is built with a fully responsive layout optimized for smaller screens. For the absolute best mobile experience and offline compatibility during tournament play, you can download the native Android app on Google Play.

Is there an iOS version of the app?

While we don't have the app on the Apple App Store, the calculator is a fully functional web app. You can install it on your iOS device in just a few taps: open the web app in Safari, tap the Share icon (square with an up arrow), and select Add to Home Screen. It will launch as a standalone app, complete with offline capability!

About the Calculator

The Pokémon Champions VGC Damage Calculator is an independent, open-source tool designed specifically for the unique mechanics and format rules of Pokémon Champions VGC.

Our tool implements the 66 Stat Points (SP) allocation engine, allowing trainers to input points, select natures, toggle Mega Evolutions, and export Showdown sets seamlessly. Our mission is to provide competitive players with a fast, reliable, and mobile-friendly simulation tool for calculations on the ladder or at live tournaments.
