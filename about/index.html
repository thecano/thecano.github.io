About Cano Labs

Built by competitive players for precise battle simulations.

Cano Labs is an independent software studio dedicated to creating high-performance tools, utilities, and applications for competitive gaming communities. We specialize in building fast, lightweight, and mathematically accurate software that assists players in preparation, theorycrafting, and live-match decision making.

Our Core Mission

In competitive Pokémon VGC, battles are often decided by single-digit damage percentages or precise speed tiers. While general-purpose calculators exist, specialized formats require custom calculations.

Our mission with the VGC Damage Calculator for Pokémon Champions is to provide a dedicated, optimized interface that natively supports the unique mechanics of the Champions league. Rather than manually translating stats or modifying source code, we built an engine that runs the calculations directly using the format's bespoke rules.

Why Pokémon Champions?

The Pokémon Champions format represents a significant shift from standard VGC rules:

  • Stat Points (SP) Engine: IVs/EVs are replaced with a direct allocation of 66 Stat Points.
  • Bespoke Limits: A cap of 32 SP per single stat ensures players make critical trade-offs between bulk and offense.
  • Dynamic Mega Evolutions: Instant evaluation of stats, typings, and abilities for Mega-evolved forms.

Because these rule modifications fundamentally alter damage calculations, standard online VGC tools produce inaccurate values. Our tool solves this problem, providing 100% accurate results instantly.

Our Team & Technical Focus

We are a small team of software developers and active VGC players. Our combined experience guides our design principles:

  • Performance First: Battle timers are short. The web and mobile apps are optimized for minimal bundle sizes and immediate load times.
  • Offline Accessibility: Tournament halls often have poor network reception. The iOS and Android applications function entirely offline.
  • Open & Community Driven: We regularly update our calculations to reflect mechanics updates and bug fixes reported by the community.
